Our Story

North Star Bakery started with one oven, one starter, and a few neighbors willing to try something new.

How We Started

North Star Bakery opened on Main Street to bring handmade bread and pastry back to the neighborhood. It began as early-morning baking out of a shared kitchen and grew into a storefront through word of mouth from commuters, families, and regulars.

We still mix our doughs in small batches, shape everything by hand, and bake in the same ovens we started with.

Today, North Star Bakery is a stop for coffee and a croissant on the way to work, a cake pickup for a birthday, or a place to order for a wedding.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

  • Local First

    We buy flour, dairy, eggs, and produce from regional mills and farms when we can.

  • Slow & Natural

    Our breads use a natural starter and slow fermentation, not shortcuts.

  • Real Ingredients

    No artificial preservatives or flavorings. If it wouldn't be in a home kitchen, it's not in ours.

  • Seasonal Menus

    Our specialty items change with the seasons, based on what's freshest and most local.
